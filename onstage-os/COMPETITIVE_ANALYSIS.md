# OnStage Competitive Analysis — Internal Strategy Doc

## Tier 1 — Direct threats

### Eventbrite (Global leader)
HAS: Discovery, ticketing, email marketing, basic analytics, mobile app
MISSING: Staff management, equipment tracking, bar/stock ops, real community, verified-attendee social, ePOS integration, SA-specific features, P&L per event
OUR EDGE: Full ops suite + owned community + SA-first + lower fees

### Quicket (SA dominant)
HAS: ZAR ticketing, local events, SA payment gateways
MISSING: EVERYTHING on the ops side. Zero staff tools, zero equipment tracking, no community, no social, no customer profiles
OUR EDGE: We are Quicket + ops + community + social. Same market, 10x the product.

### Cvent (Enterprise)
HAS: Venue sourcing, enterprise registration, detailed reporting, compliance tools
MISSING: Consumer community, mobile-first experience, affordable SMB pricing, social, live ops
OUR EDGE: Everything Cvent has for enterprise, accessible to a 200-person Cape Town club night

## Tier 2 — Partial competitors

### RA (Resident Advisor)
HAS: Premium electronic music curation, artist profiles, reviews, global discovery, credibility
MISSING: Ops tools, real ticketing (links out), organiser management tools, general events (not just electronic)
OUR EDGE: Full ops stack + all genres + real ticketing + owned community
NOTE: RA's curation model is aspirational. We should consider a verified/featured tier.

### Facebook Events
HAS: Massive reach, RSVP, event pages, groups, free
MISSING: Real ticketing (links out), ops tools, SA-specific, brand safety for nightlife, no verification
OUR EDGE: Owned platform (no algorithm risk), native ticketing, ops tools, brand-safe
NOTE: Cape Town nightlife organisers are LEAVING Facebook. We time this perfectly.

### Hopin / RingCentral Events
HAS: Virtual/hybrid events, good networking tools, enterprise
MISSING: Physical event ops, consumer community, SA market, small event support
OUR EDGE: Physical-first, ops-first, consumer community

### Ticket Tailor
HAS: Simple self-serve ticketing, good value, white-label
MISSING: Community, ops, discovery, social
OUR EDGE: Full product vs single-feature tool

## Tier 3 — Adjacent threats

### Mindbody / Tripleseat / Planning Pod
HAS: Venue management tools
MISSING: Ticketing, community, consumer app
OUR EDGE: Full stack including consumer side

### Bizzabo
HAS: B2B networking events, enterprise analytics
MISSING: Consumer community, physical ops, SMB pricing
OUR EDGE: B2C side + real-world ops

## Gaps NOBODY has filled (our moat)

1. **Verified-attendee social** — posts/photos tagged "you were actually there" (ticket = badge)
2. **ePOS-linked stock management** — consumables decline in real-time as bar sells
3. **Ops + community in one login** — organiser manages event AND talks to their fans
4. **Ticket-gated group chats** — buy a ticket = join the event community
5. **P&L per event with staff hours** — full financial picture in one place
6. **SA-first** — ZAR, local payment gateways, Lightspeed K-Series ePOS, local WhatsApp culture
7. **Custom branded event pages** — organiser logo/colours = their brand, not Eventbrite's
8. **Boost ads within the community** — promote events to our own audience, not Facebook's

## What we're building that NONE of them have

- Full ops (staff, gear, consumables, venues, P&L)
- Owned social network (feed, wall, posts, photos, aftermovies)
- Ticket-gated communities
- ePOS integration (Lightspeed K-Series)
- In-platform ad network (boost events to our community)
- Custom themes per organiser brand
- Verified attendee content
- 16:9 aftermovies + reels-format short video
- SA-specific from day one
